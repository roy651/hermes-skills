② Deterministic prescreen — 3 admission lanes no-LLM

SPY context sets required_pct_off_high. Each ticker scored on 5 criteria (off-high · >MA200 · ATR contraction · volume contraction · BB squeeze) + a $20M avg-dollar-volume liquidity floor. Three lanes admit differently:
Accumulation laneClassic base: score ≥ MIN_SCORE, full rel-perf & sector disqualifiers. The strict path.events.py
Markup-pullback laneConfirmed breakout pulling back to its LPS — bypasses the off-high floor. Ranked quietest-rally-first. Capped.MP_PRESCREEN_CAP
Early-accumulation laneMarkdown→base transition: Selling-Climax → Automatic-Rally → Secondary-Test. A >30pp underperformer is admitted only with the full SC→AR→ST sequence (the falling-knife guard). Ranked freshest-ST-first. Capped.EA_PRESCREEN_CAP

Parallel systems — holdings & watch (run on their own crons)

Exit watch weeklyexit.py --section all: holdings + watchlist full Wyckoff read + scale-out ladder. Close-based, may lag the daily stop.exit.py · ladder.py
Stop check dailyTrailing stop = tighter of chandelier (3×ATR) & structure (20d-low), anchored entry-onward. Bonds (asset_class:bond) exempt. Close-through = hard exit; intraday touch = heads-up.stop_check.py · risk.py
Watchlist tripwire dailyFires when price enters a ±1% band around a hand-seeded support/resistance in watchlist_levels. Reply-to-verify → full LLM read. Level-less names stay silent.watchlist_scan.py
Parked watch weeklyThesis-watch for names not yet entry-ready. Deterioration score (0–9); a name that stops deteriorating → promote to watchlist & seed levels.parked_scan.py · deterioration.py
Bond review monthlyRate/duration thesis read for the bond sleeve (exempt from the trailing stop). Exit = thesis change, not a price stop.bond_review.py
Portfolio value dailyMark-to-market P/L; open_date-aware (same-day fills measured from buy price, not period-start).portfolio_value.py
Trade ledgerAppend-only fills log for go-forward entry/exit performance review.trade_log.py
Ad-hoc explainFree deterministic Wyckoff narrative for any ticker — no credits. Use before re-burning an LLM read.explain.py
Shared engine. events.py (SC/AR/ST, spring, SOS, LPS, upthrust/UTAD, SOW/LPSY) and deterioration.py (0–9) feed both the deterministic prescreen/stops and the LLM prompts, so the mechanical and interpretive layers speak the same Wyckoff vocabulary. Glossary: references/wyckoff-events-glossary.md.
Infra & guardrails. All LLM calls serialize behind one claude-proxy lock (concurrency was the old degradation cause). Every new lens is deterministic-first, LLM only on survivors, confluence-only (never a standalone trigger), and cites invalidation. Nothing auto-trades. PII stays runtime-only — holdings, positions, and the (now-gitignored) config.yaml watchlist never enter git; this diagram is neutral architecture.
